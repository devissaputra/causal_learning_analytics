# Analysis Card — OULAD Causal Learning Analytics Bundle

## Purpose
Transparent observational ATE-style analysis of early assessment submission and later course outcome within one OULAD module-presentation.

## Exposure
At least one assessment submission by day 30.

## Outcome
Pass/Distinction versus Fail/Withdrawn.

## Design safeguards
Pre-treatment covariates only; explicit propensity provenance; overlap, weights, effective sample size and balance diagnostics; raw and adjusted contrasts kept separate.

## Central limitation
Unmeasured confounding remains plausible. The output must be read as a model-based observational estimate conditional on assumptions, not as proof that forcing early submission would improve outcomes.
